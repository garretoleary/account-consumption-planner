## Proven case: Shift handover synthesis

- **Problem:** Shift handovers were consuming supervisor time and leaving production context difficult to carry between teams.
- **Root causes:** Information was fragmented, handover quality varied, and supervisors spent time reconstructing events before acting.
- **Actions:** Advanced Fabrication introduced a consistent handover summary, retained human review and measured the result against a fixed baseline.
- **Value:** 110,000 shift handovers/year × $135 production-downtime impact/shift handover × 10% improvement/year = **$1,485,000 production downtime avoided/year**. The **5,500 supervisor hours saved/year** is a separate result and is not the source of the money value. Against **$480,000 usage cost/year**, customer return is **3.09×**.
